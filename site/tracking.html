How to read this. Every game gets two separate picks: a moneyline pick (who wins the game outright) and a spread pick (which side beats the betting line). They often disagree, so they are tracked separately. "Units" is profit from betting one unit on every pick at the prices the sportsbook actually posted — positive means the picks made money, negative means they lost. Spread picks need about 52.4% to break even; green is beating that, red is losing money.
